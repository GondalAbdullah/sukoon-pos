Upgrading to Sukoon 1.1.0

For the main shop computer, the one Sukoon was first installed on. About five minutes. Your sales, stock, khatas and staff accounts all carry over.

Before it changes anything, Sukoon makes its own copy of all your data. If anything goes wrong during the upgrade, it puts that copy back and does not start. Nothing is lost.

What is new

  • Several khatas on one phone number. A customer can keep separate khatas, for example for home, for his office, or for a brother he pays for. Each one has a Label, and appears as Ali Raza (Home) everywhere, including at the till. Each khata keeps its own balance and credit limit.
  • The recovery sheet now prints clearly. Please print a new one afterwards (step 6).
  • The Settings and Staff screens display properly. Some of their layout was missing in 1.0.0.

Before you start

  • Pick a quiet moment with no sale in progress at any counter. Every counter will stop working for a minute or two.
  • Use the main shop computer. The other counters only use a web browser, so they need nothing.
  • Have Sukoon-Setup-1.1.0.exe on this computer, on a USB stick or downloaded.

The upgrade

  1. Double-click Sukoon-Setup-1.1.0.exe. Windows will say "Windows protected your PC", the same as the first time. Click More info, then Run anyway. When Windows asks whether to allow changes, click Yes.
  2. Click through the installer. You don't need to close Sukoon first; the installer closes it for you. The other counters will show that they cannot connect. This is normal, and lasts until the upgrade finishes.
  3. Wait. Sukoon backs up your data, then brings it up to date. This takes a minute or two. Don't switch the computer off.
  4. Click Finish, with Open Sukoon now ticked, and sign in.
  5. Check that it all came across. Open Khata: your customers and their balances should be exactly as they were. Open Insights: today's sales should be there. Then press F5 on each of the other counters, so they reconnect.
  6. Print a new recovery sheet. Go to Settings → Recovery sheet and press Print.

Why step 6 matters

Version 1.0.0 printed the recovery key in a font where a capital I and a small l look the same, and the key uses both. If the shop computer is ever lost, that key has to be typed back in exactly, or your off-site backups cannot be opened by anyone. The new sheet shows the same key in a font where every letter is different. Your backups are not changed. Keep the new sheet away from the shop, as before, and tear up the old one.

If something goes wrong

If a message says "Sukoon is installed, but needs attention", or Sukoon does not open, your data is safe: Sukoon put back the copy it took and did not change anything. Don't delete anything and don't install again. Call the developer, and keep these two files from the main computer for them:

C:\ProgramData\Sukoon\install.log and C:\ProgramData\Sukoon\logs\sukoon.log

Using the new khatas

Go to Khata → Open a Khata and enter the same name and phone number, with a Label such as Home or Office. Sukoon will say the number already has a khata and ask "Open another Khata on it?". Click Open another Khata on this number. If the number was already confirmed on his first khata, the new one is confirmed too. A label can also be added to an existing khata from its Edit screen.
