Keep this page away from the shop.

The offsite backups are locked with this key. Anyone who has both the storage login and this key can read the shop's books — and without this key, nobody can, including the person who wrote Sukoon. If the shop's computer is lost, this page is what gets the books back.

  1. Print this page.
  2. Keep one copy somewhere safe that is not the shop — at home, or with the shop's papers.
  3. Don't photograph it, don't put it in WhatsApp, and don't keep it on the shop's computer.

This key cannot be changed once backups have been made with it: every copy already sent offsite is locked with this one.
